Generator puzzle

Turn every tile green. Clicking a tile flips it and the tiles directly above, below, left and right of it.

Clicks: 0

Each tile only needs to be clicked once, or not at all. Clicking the same tile twice cancels out. Stuck? Show solution marks the tiles to click.

Solver: press Set up board, then click each tile to flip it between red and green until the board matches your generator. Dots show exactly which tiles to click on the real one. Press Play this board to try it here.
